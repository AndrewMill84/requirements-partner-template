## Open questions and proposals
| ID | Decision needed / why owner | Classification / evidence checked | Options and recommendation / tradeoff | Scenario / consequences | Owner / priority | Status | Blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Investigate accessible evidence before asking. Explain why it does not settle the choice. Keep one authoritative `Q` record; use a linked subsection when a packet needs more detail than a row. Status is `Open`, `Proposed`, `Unresolved`, `Resolved` or `Deferred`. `Blocks` contains explicit affected IDs, `All` or `None`. Reclassify apparent decisions answered by existing authority or bounded engineering conventions. Do not invent confirmation or make deferred questions appear resolved.
